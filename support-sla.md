# Customer Support SLA

Acme Cloud Corp. offers three support tiers, matched to the Starter, Team, and
Business plans. Starter customers get community-forum support only, with no guaranteed
response time. Team customers get email support with a guaranteed first response within
24 business hours. Business customers get priority email and chat support with a
guaranteed first response within 4 hours, 24/7.

Severity is assigned by the customer at ticket creation and may be adjusted by support
staff after triage. **Critical** (complete service outage or data loss risk): Business
customers get a 30-minute first response target, any time of day. **High** (major
feature broken, no workaround): 4-hour target during business hours. **Normal** (minor
bugs, questions): 1 business day target.

Business plan customers may request a dedicated Slack Connect channel for direct access
to the support team; this is provisioned within 2 business days of request and is
staffed during the customer's local business hours only, even though ticket-based
support for that plan remains 24/7.

Refunds and service credits: if Acme fails to meet the Critical-severity response
target for a Business customer three or more times in a calendar month, that customer
is automatically credited 10% of that month's subscription fee, applied to the next
invoice. Credits are not issued retroactively for months before the failure was
reported, and must be claimed within 60 days of the qualifying month.

All support interactions are logged and retained for 2 years for quality and training
purposes, separate from the customer data retention rules described elsewhere.
