# Acme Cloud Corp. — Vacation & PTO Policy

All full-time employees accrue paid time off (PTO) at a rate of 1.75 days per month,
totaling 21 days per year. PTO accrual begins on your first day of employment and there
is no waiting period. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rated basis matching their
scheduled hours.

Unused PTO carries over into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 5 days. Any
balance beyond 5 carried-over days is forfeited on January 1st unless local law requires
otherwise. PTO does not accrue interest and has no cash value except where required by
state law upon termination.

To request time off, submit a request through the HR portal at least 5 business days in
advance for absences of 3 days or fewer, and at least 15 business days in advance for
absences longer than 3 days. Your manager will approve or deny the request within 2
business days. Requests during the last two weeks of December are approved on a
first-come, first-served basis due to high demand.

Sick leave is tracked separately from PTO and is not subject to the accrual cap. Employees
receive 8 paid sick days per year, which do not carry over. A doctor's note is required
for sick leave lasting more than 3 consecutive days.

New parents are eligible for 12 weeks of paid parental leave, separate from PTO and sick
leave, available to any employee who has completed 6 months of continuous service.
Parental leave can be taken in a single continuous block or split into two blocks within
the first year after the birth or adoption of a child.
