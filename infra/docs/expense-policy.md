# Acme Cloud Corp. — Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees may submit expense reports for business-related costs incurred while performing
their job duties. All expense reports must be submitted through the finance portal within
30 days of the expense being incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be
reimbursed except in extraordinary circumstances approved by a VP.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to $75 per day, itemized by breakfast
($15), lunch ($25), and dinner ($35). Alcohol is not reimbursable except at
client-facing dinners, where a single round of drinks may be included in the dinner cap.
Receipts are required for any single expense over $25.

Airfare must be booked in economy class for flights under 6 hours and may be booked in
premium economy for longer flights, subject to manager approval. Employees should book
through the company travel portal to take advantage of negotiated corporate rates;
booking outside the portal requires pre-approval from a manager.

Home office equipment (monitor, keyboard, chair) may be expensed up to $500 per calendar
year for fully remote employees. This budget does not roll over between years. Software
subscriptions related to job duties must be approved by your manager before purchase and
should be submitted to IT for centralized licensing where possible.

Mileage for personal vehicle use on company business is reimbursed at the current IRS
standard mileage rate. Commuting between home and your primary office is never
reimbursable. All expense reports are subject to random audit, and repeated policy
violations may result in disciplinary action.
