# Acme Cloud Corp. — New Employee Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Acme! This guide covers your first two weeks. Before your start date, IT
will ship you a laptop pre-loaded with standard tools (Slack, the internal VPN client, and
the Acme desktop sync client). If your laptop hasn't arrived 2 business days before your
start date, contact it-help@acmecloud.example immediately.

On day one, your manager will walk you through account setup: your company email, SSO
login for internal tools, and your first sync of the shared "Engineering" or "Team"
Acme folder relevant to your role. You'll also be enrolled in the required compliance
training modules (data security, code of conduct, and anti-harassment), which must be
completed within your first 10 business days.

Your first week is intentionally light on deliverables. Expect a mix of 1:1 meetings with
teammates, a walkthrough of the codebase or core systems from your onboarding buddy (a
peer assigned to help you ramp up), and self-paced reading of internal documentation.
Most new hires ship their first small change or complete their first small project by the
end of week two.

Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy — not their manager — as a
low-pressure point of contact for questions that feel too small to bring to a manager.
Buddies are expected to check in at least twice during the first two weeks.

At the 30-day mark, you'll have a check-in with your manager to discuss how ramp-up is
going and set goals for the next 60 days. At the 90-day mark, there's a slightly more
formal review covering performance expectations going forward. Neither of these is a
formal performance review in the compensation sense — those happen on the standard
bi-annual cycle described in the separate Performance Management Handbook.
